# Participant reliability and privacy fixes, 10 November 2026

- Group AI messages display roster labels P1/P2/P3, including historical messages; new messages and ideas no longer write account names into shared metadata.
- Authors can edit their group ideas during ideation, with save/cancel and error recovery.
- Mobile ballot layout no longer collapses under the task brief. Vote buttons remain reachable, cards keep their order, failed writes are shown, and submissions cannot overtake an in-flight vote.
- Timers synchronize with the server and advance using a monotonic clock. Preview changes are observed, local timestamp estimates do not arm clocks, and start/stage failures are recoverable.
- Group progress reads the server-maintained participantPublic allow-list. Private participant documents are owner/instructor-only; direct registration bypasses, duplicate ballots, changing locked ballots, and idea ownership transfers are denied.
- Lobby and pre-group messages explain group size and outstanding members. Mandatory confirmation holds are removed. The authoritative group selections remain visible on the survey, which opens immediately when the group finishes. Configured group-first sessions retain their individual phase.
- AI scope validation tightened; errors are displayed; retries after provider failure reuse the logged prompt instead of adding another copy.

## Validation
Production build; complete individual/group/survey walkthrough; group-only edit/chat/vote flow on Chrome, Edge, and WebKit at 390x664 and 667x375; one-hour device clock skew and visibility resume; 19 Firestore emulator authorization tests; phase restoration and carry-forward guards.
Firefox could not launch in the available Windows environment. WebKit tests emulate phone viewports and touch, and are not physical iPhone tests. No claim of universal device/browser coverage.

## Deployment
Backend: functions serverClock, syncParticipantPublic, ensureGroupRoster, sendAIMessage in project ideasearchlab (europe-west1). Frontend via lab-ideasearchlab and the existing site publication workflow. Firestore rules must be deployed with the same release. Already-open old clients should reload.

## Historical data
The owner explicitly chose to leave historical records unchanged. No author-name migration or private identity archive was performed. Existing shared idea/AI author-name metadata remains stored; the new UI does not display it. Names typed by participants into free-text content are not automatically redacted. Research content, ballots, demographics, and survey answers were not bulk edited.
